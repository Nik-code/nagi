# Nagi privacy policy

_Last updated: 28 October 2026_

Nagi is a Chrome extension (a new tab page, a spaces side panel and a command bar) and two Chrome themes, Oat and Sumi. This policy covers all three.

## The short version

Nagi keeps everything on your computer. It does not collect, send, sell or share any of your data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no servers.

## What Nagi reads, and why

| Data | Used for |
|---|---|
| Open tabs and tab groups (titles and addresses) | Showing your tabs and spaces in the side panel and the command bar |
| Bookmarks, browsing history and most visited sites | Search results in the command bar and the shortcuts on the new tab page |
| Site icons | Taken from Chrome's own icon cache to label tabs |

Nagi never reads the content of web pages. It has no content scripts and no access to the sites you visit.

## What Nagi stores

Your settings, favorites and archived tabs are saved with `chrome.storage.local`, which lives only in your Chrome profile on your device. Removing the extension deletes them.

## What Nagi sends

Nothing. Nagi makes no network requests of its own. Searches you type in the command bar go to your default search engine through Chrome, exactly as if you had typed them in the address bar. Links in the settings panel (GitHub, the themes) only open when you click them.

## The themes

Oat and Sumi contain only colours and images. They run no code and handle no data.

## Changes and contact

If this ever changes, this page will be updated before the extension does. Questions: open an issue at [github.com/Nik-code/nagi](https://github.com/Nik-code/nagi/issues).
